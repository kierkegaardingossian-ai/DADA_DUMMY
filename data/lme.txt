VOLUNTARISM.

Note how little we think about artificial needs and no one reads Harry Frankfurt, The Importance of What We Care About.  We consumerists seem to be very very naive voluntarists.

NOMINALIM.

NEW DISSEMINATION TECHNOLOGIES AND PATTERNS so rapidly changing the culture, how people interact, how people love and hate and go to war with each other.
